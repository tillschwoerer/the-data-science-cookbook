# Veggie Bolognese
A hearty, meat-free twist on the classic Italian pasta sauce. The caramelized red onions give it a subtle sweetness that makes it truly special.

![Bolognese](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Carleen

## Stats
- Cooking Time: ~ 30 minutes
- Servings: 3-4

## Ingredients
- 1 package veggie mince (plant-based ground meat)
- 1 small can corn
- 1 can chopped peeled tomatoes
- 1 package crème fraîche
- 2 red onions
- Sugar (a pinch, for caramelizing)
- Salt, pepper, and spices to taste

## Instructions

1. Slice the red onions into half rings and add them to a pan. Sauté over medium heat until softened.
2. Add a pinch of sugar and let the onions caramelize lightly.
3. Add the veggie mince and fry until browned. Season generously with salt and pepper.
4. Stir in the corn.
5. Pour in the canned tomatoes, then add the caramelized onions.
6. Let everything simmer for a few minutes.
7. Stir in the crème fraîche and mix well.
8. Taste and adjust seasoning — then serve!
