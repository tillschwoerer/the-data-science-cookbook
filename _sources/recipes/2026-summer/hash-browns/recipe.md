# Hash Browns

![Hash Browns](hash-browns.jpg)

Hash browns are a simple potato dish that is crispy on the outside and soft inside. I like this recipe because it is easy to make and works well for breakfast or as a small snack in the evening.

## Ingredients

- 3-4 potatoes
- 1-2 small onion
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 egg
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Oil or butter for frying

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and grate them.
2. Put the grated potatoes in a clean towel and squeeze out as much water as possible.
3. Finely chop the onion.
4. Mix the potatoes, onion, flour, egg, salt, and black pepper in a bowl.
5. Heat oil or butter in a pan.
6. Add small portions of the potato mixture to the pan and press them flat.
7. Fry each side for a few minutes until golden brown and crispy.
8. Serve hot with ketchup or any dip.
