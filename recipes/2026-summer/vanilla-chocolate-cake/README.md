# Vanilla and Chocolate Layer cake

## Description
This cake combines both vanilla and chocolate flavors in a creative layered style. 
The cake is baked in two flavors separately, cut into semi-circles, and assembled together to create a beautiful full-circle layered cake with different creams and toppings.

## Ingredients

### For the Vanilla Batter
- 1 cup flour
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla essence
- 1/2 tsp baking powder

### For the Chocolate Batter
- 1 cup flour
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1/2 tsp baking powder

### For Decoration
- Vanilla whipped cream
- Chocolate whipped cream
- Chocolate chunks
- Cocoa powder
- Fresh strawberries

---

# Instructions

## Step 1: Prepare the Vanilla Batter
1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, egg, butter, milk, vanilla essence, and baking powder.
2. Stir well until the batter becomes smooth.

## Step 2: Prepare the Chocolate Batter
1. In another bowl, mix flour, sugar, egg, butter, milk, cocoa powder, and baking powder.
2. Mix until smooth and creamy.

## Step 3: Bake the Cakes
1. Pour the vanilla batter into one cake pan.
2. Pour the chocolate batter into another cake pan.
3. Bake both cakes at 180°C for about 30–35 minutes.
4. Let both cakes cool completely.

## Step 4: Cut and Arrange the Layers
1. Cut the vanilla cake into two equal semi-circles.
2. Cut the chocolate cake into two equal semi-circles.
3. Place one vanilla semi-circle beside one chocolate semi-circle to form a full circle.
4. Repeat for the next layer and stack them carefully on top of each other.

## Step 5: Add Cream and Decoration
1. Spread vanilla whipped cream on the vanilla side of the cake.
2. Spread chocolate whipped cream on the chocolate side.
3. Add chocolate chunks on the chocolate section.
4. Sprinkle cocoa powder on top of the chocolate cream.
5. Decorate the vanilla side with fresh strawberries.

## Tips
- Chill the cake before serving for better texture.
- Use fresh strawberries for extra flavor.
- You can add extra chocolate syrup for decoration.
- Do not overmix the batter to keep the cake soft and fluffy.

## Serving Suggestion
Serve the cake cold with ice cream or hot coffee for a perfect dessert experience.
