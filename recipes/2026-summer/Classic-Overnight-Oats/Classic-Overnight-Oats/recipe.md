# Classic Overnight Oats

Classic Overnight Oats are a simple, healthy, and refreshing breakfast that can be prepared the night before. They are creamy, nutritious, and perfect for busy mornings. I love making them because they save time and still taste delicious with fresh fruits and toppings.

![image](overnightoats.avif)

Provided by: Srishma Reddy Manne

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 1 tsp chia seeds
- Honey
- Banana and berries or any fruit of your choice

## Cooking Time
5 minutes + overnight chilling
## Servings
1 person


## Instructions
1. Add oats, milk, yogurt, and chia seeds to a jar.
2. Mix well.
3. Refrigerate overnight.
4. In the morning, add banana slices and berries or any fruit
5. Serve cold and enjoy.
  
## Tips
- Add nuts for extra crunch
- Add peanut butter for extra flavor.
- Use almond milk for a vegan version.
