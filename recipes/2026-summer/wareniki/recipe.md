# Wareniki with Strawberries
Wareniki are Slavic half-moon-shaped dumplings filled with anything from potatoes to cherries. I prefer the strawberry version because it tastes like summer on a plate. Juicy, slightly tart berries tucked into a chewy dough, served with sour cream and a sprinkle of sugar. They're a bit of work to fold, but the smell of warm strawberries bursting through the dough is absolutely worth it.
