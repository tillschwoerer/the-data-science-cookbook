# Wareniki with Strawberries
Wareniki are Slavic half-moon-shaped dumplings filled with anything from potatoes to cherries. I prefer the strawberry version because it tastes like summer on a plate. Juicy, slightly tart berries tucked into a chewy dough, served with sour cream and a sprinkle of sugar. They're a bit of work to fold, but the smell of warm strawberries bursting through the dough is absolutely worth it.

## Stats
- Preparation Time: 1 h 30 min
- Cooking Time: 30 min
- Servings: 4 people (approx. 40 dumplings)

## Ingredients

**Dough**
- 800 g flour
- 3 eggs
- 150 ml milk 
- 100 ml water
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp sugar

**Filling**
- 500 g strawberries

**To serve**
- Sour cream
- Extra sugar to taste

## Instructions
- Sift the flour into a large bowl and make a well in the center.
- Add the eggs, salt, sugar, oil, water and milk. Knead for about 7–8 minutes into a soft and elastic dough.
- Cover the bowl with a towel and let it rest for 30 minutes.
- While the dough rests, wash and prepare the strawberries. Cut them into small cubes. 
- Roll the dough into a worm shape, cut off small pieces, and roll them out into flat discs.
- Place 1 teaspoon of strawberry pieces in the center of each circle. Fold the dough over into a half-moon and pinch the edges tightly to seal.
- Place the finished wareniki on a towel so they don't stick. Don't let them touch each other.
- Cook in boiling, salted water for about 5 minutes. Make sure the pot isn’t overfilled. Stir occasionally to prevent the Wareniki from sticking to the bottom of the pot. They’re done 1–2 minutes after they rise to the surface.
- Scoop them out with a slotted spoon and drain off the water. Optionally toss them with a little melted butter.
- Serve hot with sour cream and a sprinkle of sugar on top.
