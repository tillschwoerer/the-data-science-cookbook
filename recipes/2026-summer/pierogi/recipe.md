# Pierogi

Very typical Polish dish is pierogi with various fillings. Below you will find a recipe for my favorite kind of pierogi, filled with potatoes and cheese.

### Ingredients for the batter
 - 500 g of flour
 - sglass of hot water - 250 ml
 - 4 tablespoons of oil - 50 ml
 - half teaspoon of salt

### Ingredients for a filling
 - 300 g of cheese, in Germany known as Körniger Frischkäse
 - onion - 300 g
 - 500 g of boiled potatoes
 - 2 tablespoons of butter
 - half a teaspoon of salt and pepper

## Preparatory steps:
1. Boil around 700 g of potates in a salted water. This amount will result in around 500 g of boiled potatoes. Mash the boiled potatoes. Tip: still hot potatoes are easier to mash, don't wait for them to turn cold.

2. Cut the onion. Saute the onion on a pan with butter. After around 10 minutes, when the onion turns brownish, take it off of the stove.

3. Grind the cheese to a smooth texture. Tip: while buying, remember to aim for the cheese sold in triangular shaped packagings, it is known for having the best structure for pierogi filling.

4. Combine potatoes, onion and cheese. Add salt and pepper. Congrats, your pierogi filling is already done.

5. Prepare the batter. Add flour, salt, water and oil to a bowl and start mixing it. You can use mixer or mix it with your hands. At the end your batter should be even.

6. Leave the batter for around an hour. This will prevent the shrinking effect while rolling it later.

7. After that time, you can roll the batter and start making pierogi. Take a circular form (i. e. glass) and cut out circles in the batter. Place a bit of filling (amount depends on the size of the circle) in the middle and start folding the batter around the filling. You want to end up with half-circle shape in the end.

8. Boil the prepared pierogi in a salted water for 3-4 minutes after they emerge from the water.

9. Enjoy the best dish on Earth.
