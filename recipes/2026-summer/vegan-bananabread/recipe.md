# Vegan banana bread with walnuts & chocolate
For me, banana bread is the perfect easy and quick cake-like treat. My girlfriend makes the best one, and I am always happy when she bakes it for us.

![Vegan banana bread](image.jpg)

**Provided by:** Frederik

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes in preparation; 60 minutes baking time 
- Servings: 12 portions

## Ingredients
- 300 g spelt flour
- 10 g baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 2 ripe bananas
- 250 g applesauce
- 135 ml vegetable milk (vegan "like milk" alternatives are recommended)
- 150 g organic walnut kernels
- 75 g chocolate drops (for complete vegan recipe use dark chocolate)
- 1 tbsp vegetable butter
- 1 tbsp spelt flour

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C circulating air.
2. Mix the spelt flour, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
3. In a second bowl, crush two peeled bananas with a fork, creating a porridge.
4. Add the applesauce and vegetable milk to the banana porridge and mix well.
5. Gradually add the moist ingredients to the flour, creating a homogeneous mass.
6. Roughly chop the walnuts and stir in 100 g of the walnuts together with the chocolate drops. Place 50 g of the chopped walnuts aside.
7. Grease a box pan with 1 tbsp vegetable butter and knock it out with 1 tbsp spelt flour.
8. Place the dough in the box pan. Spread the last 50 g of the walnuts on the dough.
9. Bake the banana bread at 180°C circulating air on a medium rail for about 60 minutes. After about 30-40 minutes, cover with foil so that the walnuts do not burn. This can differ by the oven and could be earlier or later.  Allow to cool after baking and divide into 12 pieces.
