# Menemen

Menemen is one of my favorite Turkish dishes. It is a simple and delicious meal made with eggs, tomatoes, peppers, and spices. I like it because it is quick to prepare and perfect for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

## Stats

- Cooking Time: about 20 minutes
- Servings: 2 to 3

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 3 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 green peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped, optional
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion if you are using it, and cook until soft.
3. Add the green peppers and cook for a few minutes.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt, black pepper, paprika, and chili flakes.
5. Let everything cook until the tomatoes become soft and slightly thick.
6. Crack the eggs directly into the pan.
7. Stir gently until the eggs are cooked but still soft.
8. Serve immediately with fresh bread.

## Tips & Tricks

- Use ripe tomatoes for the best flavor.
- Do not overcook the eggs, because menemen should stay soft and juicy.
- You can add white cheese for a richer version.
- Serve it with warm bread and tea.
