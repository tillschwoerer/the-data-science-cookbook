# Pelmeni – Russian Dumplings

Pelmeni are the soul of Russian cuisine – small, hand-shaped dumplings filled with juicy minced meat. Growing up, we made them every Sunday with the whole family gathered around the table, folding dumplings together. The smell of freshly cooked pelmeni with butter and sour cream is pure nostalgia.

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 90 minutes (including resting time)
- Servings: 4 people (approx. 60 dumplings)

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 300 g all-purpose flour
- 1 egg
- 120 ml lukewarm water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vegetable oil

### Filling
- 250 g ground pork
- 250 g ground beef
- 1 medium onion, finely grated
- 2 garlic cloves, pressed
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp ice-cold water

### To Serve
- 50 g butter
- Sour cream to taste
- Fresh dill

## Instructions

- **Step 1 – Make the dough:** Mix flour and salt in a bowl. Add the egg, oil, and lukewarm water, then knead into a smooth, elastic dough (about 8–10 minutes). Wrap in cling film and let rest for 30 minutes.

- **Step 2 – Prepare the filling:** Combine ground pork and beef with the grated onion, garlic, salt, pepper, and ice-cold water. Knead well until the mixture binds together. Refrigerate until needed.

- **Step 3 – Roll out the dough:** On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough out thinly (about 2 mm). Cut out circles using a round cutter or a glass (approx. 7 cm / 2¾ in diameter).

- **Step 4 – Shape the pelmeni:** Place about 1 tsp of filling in the center of each circle. Fold the dough into a half-moon and press the edges firmly together. Bring the two ends toward each other and pinch to seal – this creates the classic pelmeni shape.

- **Step 5 – Cook:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pelmeni in batches and cook for 8–10 minutes, until they float to the surface and the dough is cooked through.

- **Step 6 – Serve:** Drain the pelmeni, toss with melted butter, and serve with sour cream and fresh dill. Enjoy immediately!

Have fun!
