# Rhubarb Tart

My Rhubarb tart is just perfect for a sunny spring weekend. The recipe comes from my Mum - so it is guaranteed to be delicious. I learned that it is important to knead the dough by hand. So, this would be my recommendation. I am convinced that it part of what makes the tart so tasty. Enjoy!

## Stats
- Preparation time: 40 minutes for the actual work + the dough should rest 1 hour in the fridge + the tarte needs 45 minutes in the oven  
- Servings: 28cm round cake pen

## Ingredients
**Dough**

- 225 g flour
- 100 g sugar
- 100 g butter
- 1 egg
- 10 g Salt

**Filling**
- 3 eggs
- 100 g sugar
- 80 g quark
- 100 g sour cream
- 600 g rhubarb

## Instructions
1. Cut the butter in small pieces and mix it under the flour. Form a ring, and add the sugar in the middle of this ring.
2. Mix the egg with the sugar. Then, more and more, knead in the flour-butter mix. Knead by hand. 
3. Let the dough rest in the fridge for 1 hour or more. Then flatten it and put it into a round cake pen.
4. For the filling, mix 3 egg yolks with the sugar. Then add quak and sour cream.
5. Beat egg white foam, cut the rhubarb in slices, and add both carefully to the rest of the filling. Pour the filling over the dough
6. Bake the rhubarb cake in the over for about 45 minutes (200° Celsius top and bottom heat)
