\# Shah Plov (Crown Pilaf)



\* \*\*Prep time:\*\* 30 mins

\* \*\*Cooking time:\*\* 2 hours

\* \*\*Servings:\*\* 6-8



\### Ingredients:

\* 3 cups Basmati rice

\* 500g lamb or beef (cubed)

\* 3 yellow onions

\* 150g butter (melted)

\* 100g dried apricots

\* 100g dried plums (albukhara)

\* 100g raisins

\* 100g chestnuts (boiled and peeled)

\* 6-8 thin lavash breads (or flour tortillas)

\* A few strands of saffron

\* Salt and black pepper to taste
