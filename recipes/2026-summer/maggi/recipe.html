Maggi Noodles#

Description#

Maggi noodles are a quick and easy instant meal that is very popular among students. It is simple to prepare, affordable, and can be customized with different flavors and ingredients. This recipe shows how to make basic Maggi noodles in just a few minutes, with optional tips to improve taste and nutrition.

Ingredients#

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles

  • 1 cup water

  • Optional: vegetables (carrot, peas, onion)

  • Optional: butter or oil

Instructions#

  1. Take a pan and pour 1 cup of water into it.

  2. Heat the water on medium flame until it starts boiling.

  3. Add the Maggi noodle cake into the boiling water.

  4. Break the noodles slightly using a spoon.

  5. Add the tastemaker (spice mix) that comes with the packet.

  6. Stir well so the masala mixes properly.

  7. Cook for about 2 minutes while stirring occasionally.

  8. If you want extra flavor, add a small amount of butter or vegetables.

  9. Turn off the heat once the noodles are soft and ready to eat.

Cooking Time#

5 minutes

Price#

Around €1 per packet

Tips#

  • Add butter or cheese for a richer taste.

  • You can add vegetables like carrots, peas, or capsicum for better nutrition.

  • Adjust water quantity if you prefer dry or soupy noodles.
