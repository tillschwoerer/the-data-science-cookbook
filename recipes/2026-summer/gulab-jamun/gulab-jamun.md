\# Gulab Jamun



!\[Gulab Jamun](gulab-jamun.jpg)



\## Description

Gulab Jamun is a popular Indian sweet made from milk solids, deep-fried until golden brown, and soaked in sugar syrup.



\---



\## Ingredients



\### For the Jamun

\- 1 cup milk powder

\- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour

\- 2 tbsp ghee

\- 1/4 cup milk

\- A pinch of baking soda

\- Oil or ghee for frying



\### For the Sugar Syrup

\- 1.5 cups sugar

\- 1.5 cups water

\- 3 cardamom pods

\- A few saffron strands (optional)

\- 1 tsp rose water



\---



\## Instructions



\### Step 1: Prepare the Sugar Syrup

1\. Add sugar and water to a pan.

2\. Heat until the sugar dissolves completely.

3\. Add cardamom and saffron.

4\. Simmer for 5 minutes and turn off the heat.

5\. Add rose water.



\### Step 2: Prepare the Dough

1\. Mix milk powder, flour, baking soda, and ghee.

2\. Add milk slowly and make a soft dough.

3\. Let the dough rest for 10 minutes.



\### Step 3: Shape and Fry

1\. Make small smooth balls without cracks.

2\. Heat oil on low-medium flame.

3\. Fry until golden brown evenly.



\### Step 4: Soak in Syrup

1\. Add fried jamuns to warm sugar syrup.

2\. Let them soak for at least 2 hours.



\---



\## Cooking Time

\- Preparation Time: 20 minutes

\- Cooking Time: 25 minutes

\- Total Time: 45 minutes



\---



\## Tips

\- Do not fry on high heat.

\- Make sure the dough is soft and smooth.

\- Let the jamuns rest in syrup for better taste.



\---



\## Serving Suggestion

Serve warm or chilled with vanilla ice cream.
