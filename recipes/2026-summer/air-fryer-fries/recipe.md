# Air Fryer Fries - Crispy Game Night Snack

Air fryer fries are a simple and reliable option for a relaxed soccer or NFL evening. Made from fresh potatoes, they turn out crispy on the outside and soft inside with minimal oil. Served with curry ketchup, mayonnaise, and homemade herb quark, this is a classic snack setup.

**Provided by:** Ethan Vetter the Air Fryer Chef

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 25 minutes  
- Servings: 1 large portion

## Ingredients

### Fries
- 200 g fresh potatoes  
- Olive oil spray  
- French fries seasoning  

### Toppings & Dips
- Hela curry spiced ketchup  
- Mayonnaise  
- 150 g low-fat quark  
- Fresh herbs (e.g. parsley, chives)  
- Salt and pepper  

## Instructions

- **Step 1 - Prepare the potatoes:** Wash the potatoes thoroughly and remove rough spots. Cut into evenly sized strips.

- **Step 2 - Season:** Place fries in a bowl, spray lightly with olive oil, and mix with french fries seasoning.

- **Step 3 - Air fry:** Preheat air fryer to 180°C (356°F). Cook for 15-20 minutes, shaking halfway through, until golden and crispy.

- **Step 4 - Make the herb quark:** Mix low-fat quark with chopped herbs, salt, and pepper. Adjust texture with a little water if needed.

- **Step 5 - Serve:** Plate the fries and serve with curry ketchup, mayonnaise, and herb quark on the side.

Enjoy!
