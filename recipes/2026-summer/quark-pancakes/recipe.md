# Quark Pancakes
I’m not entirely sure if “quark” is the correct English word, but it’s a creamy fresh dairy product. My grandma loved quark pancakes, and I like them best with Nutella:)

Provided by: Bente

## Stats
- Cooking Time: 10-15 minutes

## Ingredients
- 100 g sugar 
- 4 eggs
- 500 g quark
- 150 g flour
- 1 pinch of salt

## Instructions
- Simply mix everything together.
- Fry on both sides until golden brown.
